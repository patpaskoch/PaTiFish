# PaTiFish

A simple fishing helper for World of Warcraft: Forever. **One click = one action**: PaTiFish never casts, reels in,
equips or applies anything by itself.

## Features

- **Minimap button:** one click puts your fishing pole on (your weapons are remembered), the next one puts those
  weapons back on.
- **Fishing bar** (bottom centre, movable, shown while the pole is in your hands): fishing skill, lure time left,
  a cast bar, and the buttons **Cast**, **Lure** (your best lure onto the pole), **Catches** and **Weapons**.
- **Catch list:** everything you fished so far with its count, left of the bar.
- **Louder splash:** while fishing, effects go up and music/ambience down; your volumes come back afterwards.
- **Flashing frame** in the last seconds of a cast, so you know to cast again.
- **Reel in without aiming:** bind WoW's "Interact with Target" key and turn on "Soft interact" in the settings —
  then press the key when the bobber splashes.

WoW does not tell addons when a fish bites, so catching stays your click (or your Interact key).

## Commands

`/pfish` or `/patifish` — alone: show/hide · `settings` · `test` · `show` · `hide` · `lock` · `unlock` · `reset` ·
`debug` · `diagnose` (list the events of the next cast) · `version`

Key bindings: Key Bindings > PaTiFish (Cast, Lure, Pole on / weapons back).
